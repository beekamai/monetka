Monetka is a simple monthly money tracker modelled on the "Monthly budget" spreadsheet, only quicker and in your pocket. The interface is in Russian.

🔒 YOUR MONEY, YOUR BUSINESS
No accounts, no cloud, no ads, no analytics. The app never goes online: everything stays on your phone. Backups are one tap away and stay in your hands.

📊 EVERYTHING ON THE FIRST SCREEN
• Balance at month end and how much your savings grew
• Where the money went — a donut chart by category
• Spending and income by day
• Six-month trend and the share of income you keep

🎯 PLAN VS ACTUAL
• A plan for every category, overridable for any month
• Overspending is obvious at a glance
• A savings jar counts as money set aside, not spent; tips are tracked separately

⚡ FAST ENTRY
• The category is pre-selected, recent descriptions are one tap away
• Do the maths right in the amount field: 350+120
• "Repeat today" for regular expenses
• Deleted by mistake? Undo

📥 MOVE FROM GOOGLE SHEETS
Download your "Monthly budget" sheet as .xlsx and open it in the app: transactions, the starting balance and plans come along. Importing again creates no duplicates. Any month exports back to Excel.

✨ NICE TO USE
• Two styles, minimal and glass, in light and dark
• Swipe between tabs, months and sections
• Smooth animations that respect the system "Remove animations" setting
